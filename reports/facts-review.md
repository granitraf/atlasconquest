# Facts review

Every curated fact in `public/data/facts.json`, grouped by country. Edit that file to change, add or remove one.

- **R** is recognition: 3 = known worldwide, 2 = known to a well-read player, 1 = known mostly inside the country. Only R2 and R3 facts are asked.
- **hook** marks the memorable facts chosen to give the country its hook.
- **CHECK** marks facts I am less than fully sure about. They are listed together first.
- Facts with their own wrong options show them; the rest borrow answers to the same kind of fact about other countries.
